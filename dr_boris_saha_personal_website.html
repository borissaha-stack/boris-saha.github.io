About Me

Dr. Boris Saha is a dental professional and Consultant Endodontist with academic and clinical experience in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics. He completed his B.D.S. in 2012 and M.D.S. in 2020 from Aligarh Muslim University.

His approach combines careful diagnosis, contemporary endodontic techniques and patient-centred treatment planning.
